# Sunlight Beam: Vampire Light Hunter — QS Play listing

## Title

Sunlight Beam: Vampire Light Hunter

## Short description (tagline, 92 characters)

Hold back the vampire horde with a UV flashlight. Burn them in the light, survive the night!

## Full description

The night is pitch black, and the vampires are coming. Your only weapon is a UV flashlight: whatever it lights up starts to burn.

Aim the beam, sweep the darkness and turn vampires to ash before they reach you. Switch to the focused laser to melt tough targets from afar, but watch your battery: when it runs dry, the light goes out and only their glowing eyes remain.

Every night brings a bigger horde. Vampires drop cash, health and battery boosters — the rarer the booster, the brighter it shines. Spend your bounty in the shop on stronger flashlights: from the Standard UV Wand to the Supernova Hyper-Beam. Each new light draws out a new kind of enemy, from Shade Swarms that strike from every side to Mirror Wraiths that reflect your beam.

Every third night, the Vampire Lord rises. He charges, summons his swarm, spits blood and teleports, learning a new trick each time he returns. Dodge his dash, then hit him while he is stunned for double damage.

How many nights can you survive?

Features:
- Fast twin-stick action with a light-and-darkness twist
- 5 flashlights with a wide beam and a focused laser
- 7 enemy types, each with its own behavior, plus the Vampire Lord
- A boss fight every 3rd night that grows tougher each time
- Endless nights with steadily rising difficulty
- Synthesized music and sound effects
- Plays on desktop and mobile, no sign-in required

## How to play

Survive as many nights as you can. Light burns vampires, so keep the beam on them and don't let them reach you. Collect boosters, buy better flashlights between nights and defeat the Vampire Lord every third night.

## Controls

Desktop:
- WASD / Arrow keys — move
- Mouse — aim the light
- F or mouse click — toggle the focused laser
- B — flashlight shop
- P / Esc — pause
- M / N — music / sound on or off
- Shift+F — fullscreen

Mobile:
- Left thumb — move
- Right thumb — aim the light
- LASER button — toggle the focused laser
- Pause button — pause, shop and settings
